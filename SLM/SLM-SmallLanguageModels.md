# Are small language models the future?

Small language models (SLMs) are not replacing large language models (LLMs), 
but they are becoming the dominant force for enterprise workflows, edge devices, 
and agentic AI systems. 

[1] (https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02153?), 
[2] (https://hackernoon.com/small-language-models-have-a-trillion-dollar-future)

## Why SLMs are Gaining Ground

1. Cost and Efficiency: 
SLMs (typically under 10 billion parameters) cost 10 to 30 times less to run than massive 
frontier models while delivering up to 3.5x higher throughput. 

[1] (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.02153), 
[2] (https://ctomagazine.com/small-language-models-enterprises/), 
[3] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9xgRTznP21E&t=43)

2. Agentic Workloads: 
Research from NVIDIA and industry deployments show that 40% to 70% of routine enterprise tasks 
such as 

- classification, 
- data extraction, and 
- tool-calling 

can be handled by SLMs with no drop in performance. 

[1] (https://uk.investing.com/analysis/the-future-of-ai-is-small-language-models-200621414), 
[2] (https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02153?), 
[3] (https://ctomagazine.com/small-language-models-enterprises/)

- Speed and Low Latency: 
Smaller models respond fast enough to run locally on smartphones, personal computers, and 
specialized edge hardware. 

[1] (https://devopslearning.medium.com/why-small-language-models-are-the-future-of-agentic-ai-57ef54a4648d), 
[2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9xgRTznP21E&t=43)

- Data Sovereignty: 
Running compact models on-premises helps organizations comply with strict local data privacy and jurisdictional regulations. 

[1] (https://ctomagazine.com/small-language-models-enterprises/)

## The Bifurcated Future

- SLMs for Specialization: 
The industry is shifting toward heterogeneous systems where specialized SLMs handle specific, repetitive jobs 
(like reading invoices or writing structured JSON). 

[1] (https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/why-small-language-models-becoming-real-engine-behind-agentic-ocwbf), 
[2] (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9xgRTznP21E&t=43), [3] (https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.02153?)

- LLMs for Frontier Reasoning: Massive LLMs remain essential for complex multi-domain synthesis, deep scientific research, and open-ended creative tasks. 

[1] (https://hackernoon.com/small-language-models-have-a-trillion-dollar-future)
